COOKIES

WHAT IS A COOKIE?

Acookie is a small text file that is stored by your browser (for example Internet Explorer or Safari) on your device (for example on your computer or phone). They enable websites to store user information such as preferences and settings. In principle, cookies enable websites to remember users and their preference settings for that particular website, so that the website can respond accordingly and can gain more insight into regular visitors of the website.

HOW DOES HOLLAND DIERVOEDERS B.V. USE COOKIES?

The information that the cookies provide can help us to analyse our visitors profile and gives us the opportunity to offer you a better user experience. Certain parts of the Holland Diervoeders B.V. website use cookies for specific reasons (for example for the proper operation of a function for the display of images on your device).
What types of cookies are there?
We differentiate between different types of cookies:

  • Functional cookies: These are necessary for a proper operation of the website. They ensure, for instance, that the desired information is shown quickly and correctly each time you visit our website. No consent is needed for placing functional cookies.
  • Analytical cookies: We use these to analyse how our website is used. For example how often our website is visited, on which page you enter our website and the areas on our website that are visited most frequently. No consent is needed for placing these cookies because there are no privacy consequences.
  • Tracking cookies: These are cookies that can follow your surfing behaviour. That way we and advertisers can show personalised online ads and tailor-made content based on your surfing behaviour. We ask for you consent for placing these cookies.
  • Social media plug-in cookies: Social media plug-in cookies are used to show social media content on our website. We ask for your consent for placing these cookies.

We use both session-cookies (disappear when the web browser is closed) and permanent cookies (remain on your device unless they are deleted). Below you will find an overview of the cookies we use.

It is possible that, during your visit to Holland Diervoeders B.V.s website, there are a number of active cookies that have nothing to do with Holland Diervoeders B.V. or its suppliers.
