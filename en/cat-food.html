Find the right food for your cat

With Voskes, we like to dish up a healthy and tasty menu for your cat. Our range of complementary wet food and high-quality wet and dry food ensures your cat gets the right ingredients, without artificial colours or flavours.
Whether you have a kitten, adult cat or senior, our cat food is carefully formulated with specific ingredients to meet the needs of each stage of your cat's life. And taste is an essential, leading factor. Some good food and tasty treats every cat deserves!
