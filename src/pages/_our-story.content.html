Voskes,
since 1981

Voskes has been known since 1981 for its many types of healthy snacks. For more than 40 years, dogs and cats have loved the delicious and honest taste of our treats... and that immediately formed the perfect meow-ment for us to expand the existing range to a complete range of snacks and food.

Surprising

Voskes offers more than 200 different cat & dog snacks and food, always keeping up with the latest trends. Plenty of choice to surprise your pet every day!

Responsible

With the development of our products, pet welfare comes first. All is tailored to the specific needs of cats and dogs.

Quality

Our Voskes delicacies are developed with the greatest care and we ensure that only high-quality ingredients are used during the production process.

Happiness

Voskes loves animals. We aim for a happy pet with our snacks and food. After all, we all want the best for our housemates, and a happy pet also means a happy pet-owner!
